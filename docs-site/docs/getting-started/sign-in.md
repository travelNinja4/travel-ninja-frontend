# Sign in and out

Everything in Travel Ninja is behind a sign-in, so only the agency's own team can see its tours, bookings and guests.

**Who uses it:** everyone at the agency with an account · **Where to find it:** opening Travel Ninja takes you to the sign-in page

![Sign in to your agency: Work email, Password, Remember me, Forgot password? and Sign in](/img/screens/getting-started/sign-in.png)

## How to sign in

1. Enter your **Work email** and **Password**. Click the eye icon to check what you typed.
2. Tick **Remember me** if you're on your own device and want to stay signed in after closing the browser.
3. Click **Sign in**.

A green **You've logged in successfully!** message appears and the [Dashboard](../user-guide/dashboard.md) opens.

## Staying signed in

- With **Remember me** ticked, you stay signed in on that device even after closing the browser.
- Without it, you stay signed in until you close the browser.
- If your sign-in has ended and you open any Travel Ninja page, you're taken back to the sign-in page.

Don't tick **Remember me** on a shared or public computer.

## How to sign out

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the side menu. You're returned to the sign-in page. See [Finding your way around](../user-guide/navigation.md).

## Business rules

- **Work email** must be a valid email address, up to 50 characters.
- **Password** must be at least 6 characters before the form will send it. Travel Ninja then checks it against the account.
- Opening Travel Ninja's home address always takes you to the sign-in page.

## Messages you might see

| Message                         | What it means                                                                         | What to do                                                        |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Please enter email address**  | The email box is empty                                                                | Enter your work email                                             |
| **Invalid email address**       | The email isn't in a valid format                                                     | Check for typos                                                   |
| **Please enter valid password** | The password is shorter than 6 characters                                             | Enter your full password                                          |
| **Error!** with a reason        | Travel Ninja couldn't sign you in, for example because the email or password is wrong | Check your details, or [reset your password](./reset-password.md) |

## Things to check when testing

- [ ] An empty form shows **Please enter email address** and **Please enter valid password**.
- [ ] A badly formatted email shows **Invalid email address**.
- [ ] Wrong details show an **Error!** message and you stay on the sign-in page.
- [ ] Correct details show **You've logged in successfully!** and open the Dashboard.
- [ ] Without **Remember me**, closing and reopening the browser asks you to sign in again.
- [ ] With **Remember me**, you're still signed in after reopening the browser.
- [ ] Opening a page such as Tours while signed out goes to the sign-in page.
- [ ] **Sign out** returns to the sign-in page, and opening the Dashboard afterwards asks you to sign in again.
